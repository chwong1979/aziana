Parking at Aziana

Everything worth knowing before you arrive — so the only surprise of the evening is on the plate.

The car park belongs to the marina

Aziana sits inside Bobby's Marina, on the water. It is one of the loveliest settings on the island to have dinner — and it means the parking is the marina's, not ours.

Bobby's Marina owns and runs the car park. They provide it as a paid, secured service, with staff on the gate. Aziana is a tenant of the marina like any other business here: we do not own the car park, we do not set its prices, and we receive nothing from what you pay to park. We are telling you all of this because we would much rather you knew where you stood before you arrived than found out at the barrier on your way home.

What we have done is make an arrangement with the marina so that our diners are not paying to sit down with us.

What Aziana covers for you

Two hours of validated parking, on us. When you dine with us in the evening, bring your parking ticket to your table and we will stamp it. Those two hours are paid for by Aziana as a thank you for choosing us.

6pm to 6am

Validation is an evening arrangement, and a stamp is only good inside that window. If you arrive before 6pm, the time before 6pm is yours to settle.

Two hours

Two hours is a comfortable dinner. Beyond that you settle at the gate — and in the evening the marina's rate drops to $1 an hour per car, down from $2 in the daytime.

One stamp per $25

$25 on the bill is one ticket stamped, $50 is two, $75 is three — so a table that arrived in two cars is covered the same way.

The $25 is our own house rule rather than the marina's, and it is only there to stop the arrangement being used by somebody buying a single drink. In practice we are not strict about it. If you are a little under, please just ask — we would far rather stamp your ticket than count your bill.

Coming as a group, or for a party?

It is worth emailing the marina ahead of time to ask what they can do for you.

dock@bobbysmarina.com

Bobby's Marina handles parking for the whole property, and for a group booking or an event it is always worth asking them directly whether anything can be arranged. Our validation is designed for tables of diners, and we genuinely are not able to stamp for every guest arriving at a large party. One email ahead of time saves everybody a queue at the end of a good night.

Please don't lose your ticket

A lost parking ticket carries a $40 fee to exit the car park. This is the marina's policy and there is nothing we can do about it from our side, so please keep it somewhere safe — your pocket, your bag, or on the table beside you while we stamp it.
